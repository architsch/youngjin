Dynamic Doors

Author: Youngjin Kang & Claude   Date: August 30, 2026

New here? Start with What is ThingsPool?

ThingsPool, a 3D virtual universe of rooms, now includes custom doors!

Try entering these new doors, and you will find yourself wandering off into places of pure mystery.

Each door has its own name, and it leads to a unique site of adventure. Try entering them one by one, and you will end up navigating ThingsPool's vast labyrinth of rooms.

Internally, we now have an admin system which grants the special admin user (i.e. the "Game Master") a right to install these custom doors, connect them to other rooms, and even create brand new rooms!

So come and enjoy this new, ever-growing world of interconnected doors.
