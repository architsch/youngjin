Introducing Robot Rooms

Author: Youngjin Kang   Date: August 8, 2026

New here? Start with What is Robot Rooms?

Robot Rooms is a 3D chat app you can open up in a browser. You don't need to download anything; just click the link and you will be right inside our virtual universe!

Robot Rooms is made up of virtual rooms, where you can chat with people, build your own personal space, and partake in unforeseen adventures. So jump in, and become part of our everlasting journey.

You can even have your own room and fill it with various treatures.

This is the first post of Robot Rooms' development log, which is a news feed for the project's major updates. Whenever a new feature comes in, you will hear about it here. Stay tuned!
