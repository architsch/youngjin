Restricted Zones

Author: Youngjin Kang & Claude   Date: September 5, 2026

New here? Start with What is ThingsPool?

ThingsPool, a freely editable virtual world, now supports restricted zones.

A restricted zone is a special part of your room that nobody but you can touch - the walls, the floor, the paintings, and every other treasure you have gathered there.

The rest of the room stays open, so you can hand your friends a corner to build in while your own private area stays intact. They can walk right through your zone and admire everything in it, but not a single block of it will move!

So come and claim a corner of ThingsPool for your own.
