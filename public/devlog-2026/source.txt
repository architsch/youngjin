@@<h3>New here? Start with <a class="inlineButton" href="https://thingspool.net#what-is-thingspool">What is ThingsPool?</a></h3>

ThingsPool is a 3D chat app you can open up in a browser. You don't need to download anything; just click the link and you will be right inside our virtual universe!

<devlog-intro-editing>

ThingsPool is made up of virtual rooms, where you can chat with people, build your own personal space, and partake in unforeseen adventures. So jump in, and become part of our everlasting journey.

You can even have your own room and fill it with various treatures.

This is the first post of ThingsPool's development log, which is a news feed for the project's major updates. Whenever a new feature comes in, you will hear about it here. Stay tuned!

[Game Modes] August 15, 2026

@@<h3>New here? Start with <a class="inlineButton" href="https://thingspool.net#what-is-thingspool">What is ThingsPool?</a></h3>

ThingsPool, an immersive browser-based metaverse app, now supports two distinct game modes - "Play Mode" and "Edit Mode".

<game-mode-play>

In Play Mode, you will be engaging in all sorts of rich gameplay experiences - such as inspecting, attacking, picking things up, eating, and so on.

<game-mode-character>*

In Edit Mode, on the other hand, you will be editing the room's walls, floors, ceilings, doors, staircases, furniture, and a variety of other hidden corners.

<game-mode-editing>

Enter the Edit Mode, and click anything you see; you will fly in mid air, chasing what you are editing in real time!

[Second Floor] August 21, 2026

@@<h3>New here? Start with <a class="inlineButton" href="https://thingspool.net#what-is-thingspool">What is ThingsPool?</a></h3>

You can now build two-story rooms in ThingsPool!

ThingsPool is a 3D real-time chat application, in which you can build your own room, socialize with other people, and engage in many hidden adventures.

<second-floor-stairs>

ThingsPool used to have only single-story rooms, which severely limited your creative freedom. It is no longer the case. Rooms are now two stories high, meaning you can build stairs, vast halls of high ceilings, and other architectural wonders!

<second-floor-gallery>

Come join ThingsPool. The second floor's open air is inviting you.
