Lights, Sky, and Fog!

Author: Youngjin Kang   Date: September 13, 2026

New here? Start with What is ThingsPool?

ThingsPool now has lights!

Inside ThingsPool's vast sandbox environment, you are now able to install lamps, change their colors, and experiment with their chromatic harmony.

Try combining different lights, until you end up creating your own visual masterpiece.

Besides, rooms are now furnished with atmospheric effects (e.g. fog), sky, and the horizon. Look out the window, and embrace the vastness of our natural landscape!
