combination of 5 different single sounds: 2 Sounds of a printer, once being open and once being closed. A box full of
rubbish being shaked, a ratchet and a small bell." CC0 requires no credit; it is given here because it is deserved.

Cut from Freesound's high-quality MP3 preview of it (the original's download asks for an account): 0.085 s to 1.55 s,
its last 0.35 s faded out, set to a common loudness with its peak 1 dB below full scale, and saved as 16-bit mono WAV at
44.1 kHz (its two channels are the same). The audio manifest names the clip it is (see `docs/audio/audio.md`).
