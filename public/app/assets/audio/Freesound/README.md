Each sound's own page names the license, and none of them is in a remix group. CC0 requires no credit; it is given
here because it is deserved. Each was cut from Freesound's high-quality MP3 preview of it (the original's download asks
for an account), set to a common loudness with its peak at least 1 dB below full scale, and saved as 16-bit mono WAV at
the preview's sample rate. The audio manifest names the clip each one is (see `docs/audio/audio.md`).

## `cashRegister.wav`

Its author made it from recordings of their own: "It's not the sound of an actual cash register but the
combination of 5 different single sounds: 2 Sounds of a printer, once being open and once being closed. A box full of
rubbish being shaked, a ratchet and a small bell."

Cut: 0.085 s to 1.55 s, its last 0.35 s faded out, at 44.1 kHz (its two channels are the same).

## `cannonShot.wav`

Its author's description: "Small air cannon. Compressed air through length of pipe From special effects shoot. No
debris. Take one of three, half-speed."

Cut: 0.0924 s to 0.5424 s, its last 0.171 s faded out, at 48 kHz (a mono recording).

## `powderBurst.wav`

Its author's description: "A fuse with a small explosion done with some gunpowder."

Cut: the explosion alone, 2.681 s to 3.181 s, its last 0.2 s faded out, at 44.1 kHz (its two channels are the same).

## `gearGrind.wav`

Its author's description: "Grinding gears and steady clinking of an antique, hand-cranked drill press."

Cut to loop: the stretch from 27.9632 s to 28.7033 s, between two of its clinks, with its last 0.05 s crossfaded into
its first 0.05 s, which leaves 0.69 s that ends where it begins, at 44.1 kHz (its two channels are the same).

## `rubbleDrop.wav`

Its author's description: "Several concrete bits being dropped from 4 feet onto asphalt, recorded on a Zoom H5-XY and
denoised with Izotope RX 11".

Cut: the last of its drops, 6.1724 s to 6.6524 s, its last 0.168 s faded out, at 44.1 kHz (its two channels averaged).
