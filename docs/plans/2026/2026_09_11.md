# New Room Settings Adjustments

## Sky and Fog should have Separate Colors

Right now, the skybox's sky color and the fog color are identical (see @src/client/graphics/graphicsManager.ts ). While this is a great way of establishing synchronicity between the sky and fog, which lets us exclude the fog-rendering logic from the skybox without introducing too much chromatic mismatch between the sky and the indoor space's fog effect, it also limits the degree of freedom when it comes to color customization. Some users may want to make the sky's color distinct from that of the fog.

- In order for this to work, the skybox mesh should be rendering the fog based on its pixel's estimated distance from the camera (since the opacity of the fog depends on the distance). We can define this "estimated distance" as the distance between:
  1. The camera's position, and
  2. The point of intersection between the camera's line of sight and the room's boundary plane (i.e. one of the following: (x = 0), (x = NUM_VOXEL_COLS), (z = 0), (z = NUM_VOXEL_ROWS)).

- I guess the aforementioned rendering logic may be cleanly implemented if we render the skybox mesh using two shader passes: (1) one for rendering the sky, clouds, and ground, and (2) another one for rendering the fog over it.

- By rendering both the sky/clouds/ground AND the fog on the skybox mesh, we will be able to let the sky and fog have different colors while also ensuring that they will blend naturally no matter where the camera is in world-space. For example, suppose that there is a window (hole) on the room's boundary wall. If the camera is standing right in front of the window and is looking at the sky through it, the fog will hardly be visible anyways and so the fog's color won't interfere with that of the sky. If the camera is standing far away from the window and is looking at the sky through it, the presence of the fog's color on both the indoor space AND the skybox will make sure that the fog's color is seamlessly spread throughout the whole room and not awkwardly cut off right on the window's aperture.

- On the DB side, we will need to add a new attribute to the room's `prefs` field for representing the "sky color". The sky shader, then, will need to render the sky's color using this new color attribute instead of just borrowing from the fog's color.

- On the UI side, we will need to rename `cloudsPanel.tsx` into `skyPanel.tsx` and replace its "Color" input field with 2 separate color input fields: "Sky Color" and "Cloud Color".

## Fog's Near and Far Ranges Must Stay In Order

Inside `fogPanel.tsx`, you will see two range input components: one for `fogNearStep`, and the other one for `fogFarStep`. Currently, it is possible to make `fogNearStep` greater than `fogFarStep` (which doesn't yield a sensible result). So, please add a UI logic which automatically adjusts the input-values of these two range input components so as to ensure that `fogNearStep` is always less than or equal to `fogFarStep`.

## Wider Speed Range for the Noise Shaders

The adjustable parameters in `customizeRoomPanel.tsx`, which are responsible for controlling the speed of the smoke & clouds, must have much greater maximum values, so as to let us create noises which are moving extremely fast.
